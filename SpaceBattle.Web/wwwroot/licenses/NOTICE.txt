Space Battle Web distribution

This browser application bundles only SECS, SpaceBattle.Core, SpaceBattle.SECS,
Aether.Physics2D, .NET/Blazor WebAssembly, and Lucide icons. It does not bundle
LeoECS, LeoECS Lite, Morpeh, StaticEcs, Silk.NET, or the benchmark/test tooling.
